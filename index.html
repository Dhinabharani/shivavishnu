Our Story

Tamil Nadu's Trusted Land Partners Since 2009

Shiva Vishnu Real Estate was founded with a single mission: to make land ownership simple, transparent, and accessible for every Tamil Nadu family. What began as a small office in Chennai has grown into one of the most trusted real estate firms across 6 major districts.

We believe that buying land is one of the most important decisions of your life. That's why we offer end-to-end support — from shortlisting to registration — with complete transparency at every step.

Our expertise spans residential plots, agricultural land, commercial sites, and farm land across Chennai, Chengalpattu, Thiruvarur, Vellore, Viluppuram, and Kanchipuram.
